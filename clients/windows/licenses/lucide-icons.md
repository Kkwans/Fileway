# Lucide Icons

Source: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/1ef60af944de207e0d5176905a0759ba12e6ee8d

Original SVG strokes are rendered to transparent PNG for native ImageIcon support. Light and dark variants preserve the original geometry and stroke width. Reproduce with eng/Import-LucideIcons.cjs and sharp. ISC / MIT notices are in Lucide-LICENSE.txt.
